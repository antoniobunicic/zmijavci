01 — Welcome

Zmijavci sits on the edge of the Imotski field, where fertile soil meets limestone. A place of vineyards, slow mornings and traces reaching back to early Christianity.

Landmarks in Zmijavci

Early Christian Basilica

Heritage

Late antique foundations with a cross-shaped baptismal font.

Zmijavci Bridge

Built heritage

An arched stone bridge over the Vrljika river.

All Saints Parish Church

Sacred heritage

A stone church with a tall bell tower in the heart of the village.

The Cross

Viewpoint

A white cross on the hill above the village, overlooking the Imotski field.

04 — Food & drink

Flavours of the Imotski region

Hinterland cooking is simple and honest: what the fields, vineyards and flocks provide, cooked slowly under the peka bell or over an open fire.

01

Imotska torta

An almond cake, the centrepiece of every Imotski celebration.

02

Kujundžuša

An indigenous white grape of the Imotski field.

03

Lamb under the peka

Slow-cooked under embers, with potatoes.

04

Pršut and sir iz mišine

Cured ham and sheepskin-aged cheese, a hinterland classic.

05 — Day trips

Day trips around the Imotski region

Karst lakes, fortresses above the field and a mountain that drops into the sea — all a short drive from Zmijavci.

Day trip map →
01Nature

Blue Lake

A karst lake on the edge of Imotski. When it dries out in summer, football is played on its bed.

02Nature

Red Lake

A deep karst sinkhole with steep reddish cliffs, one of the deepest in Europe.

03Culture

Topana Fortress

A fortress above Imotski looking straight down into the Blue Lake and across the whole field.

04Active

Biokovo

A nature park with the Skywalk viewpoint and Sveti Jure peak, looking out over the islands.

05Nature

Prološko blato

Wetlands near Proložac, a refuge for birds and quiet walks.

06Active

Vrljika River

A sinking river winding through the Imotski field — for cycling or a walk by the water.
