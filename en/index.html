Welcome

Zmijavci holds a unique story of landscape, history and heritage. From an Early Christian basilica and medieval stećci to stone bridges and watermills on the Vrljika.

Landmarks in Zmijavci

Early Christian Basilica

The remains of a 5th–6th century church complex with two baptismal fonts.

Zmijavci Bridge

A six-arched stone bridge over the Vrljika from the early 19th century.

All Saints Church

An 1895 parish church with a wooden altar and stained glass by Branimir Dorotić.

Todorića gradina

A viewpoint over the Imotski plain with a fifteen-metre votive cross.

Stećci at Brižine

A medieval cemetery of at least 31 stećci on a prehistoric burial mound.

Stećci at Dikovača

Eight decorated medieval stećci on a 290-metre hilltop.

Bublin Bridge

A late 19th-century stone bridge beside the historic Petrović watermill.

Đogić Watermill

A stone watermill on the Vrljika with a wooden water wheel.

Flavours of the Imotski region

Hinterland cooking is simple and honest: what the fields, vineyards and flocks provide, cooked slowly under the peka bell or over an open fire.

Day trips

Day trips around the Imotski region

Karst lakes, fortresses above the field and a mountain that drops into the sea — all a short drive from Zmijavci.

Day trip map →
01Nature

Blue Lake

A karst lake on the edge of Imotski. When it dries out in summer, football is played on its bed.

02Nature

Red Lake

A deep karst sinkhole with steep reddish cliffs, one of the deepest in Europe.

03Culture

Topana Fortress

A fortress above Imotski looking straight down into the Blue Lake and across the whole field.

04Active

Biokovo

A nature park with the Skywalk viewpoint and Sveti Jure peak, looking out over the islands.

05Nature

Prološko blato

Wetlands near Proložac, a refuge for birds and quiet walks.

06Active

Vrljika River

A sinking river winding through the Imotski field — for cycling or a walk by the water.
