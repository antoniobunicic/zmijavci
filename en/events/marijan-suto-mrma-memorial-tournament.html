Marijan Šuto Mrma Memorial Tournament

On the last day of July, Zmijavci hosts a memorial football tournament honouring a Croatia Zmijavci player and Croatian defender who fell in the Homeland War.

Date
31 July 2027
Location
Zmijavci
Category
Sport

About the tournament

The tournament bears the name of Marijan Šuto Mrma, a Croatia Zmijavci player and Croatian defender who fell in the Homeland War. Every year it brings together teams and fans who keep his memory alive through football.
