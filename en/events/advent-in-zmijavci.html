Advent in Zmijavci

From 19 December to 3 January, the centre of Zmijavci turns into a winter fairy tale — an ice rink, Advent stalls and thousands of lights.

Dates
19 Dec 2026 – 3 Jan 2027
Location
Zmijavci, village centre
Category
Winter

About the event

Advent in Zmijavci brings together locals and visitors from across the Imotski region. The village centre glows under illuminated stars, and the whole family gathers around the ice rink, the marquee and the Advent stalls.

The illuminated sign and decorated trees set the scene for winter evenings to remember.

Most Beautiful Advent in Croatia

Advent in Zmijavci won first place in the municipality category of the 2025/2026 Most Beautiful Advent in Croatia selection by the Načelnik.hr portal.
